# Alined Support

Thanks for using Alined.

Alined is a local-first app for tracking activity, planner items, expenses, nutrition, counters, rhythm blocks, ideas, and day-level notes in one place. Alined Expenses is the Android expense-focused version of Alined if made available.

## Helpful Details to Include

If you contact support, it helps to include:

- the device model you are using
- your iOS version
- the app version
- what you were trying to do
- what happened instead
- whether the issue can be repeated

If your question is about Alined Expenses, include your Android version as well.

## Alined Plus

Alined Plus is an optional subscription that unlocks advanced tools such as custom Home layout, advanced Planner tools, Counters, Rhythm, Ideas, and advanced review graphs.

To view Alined Plus in the app:

More → Alined Plus

If you subscribed but Plus is not unlocked, open the Alined Plus screen and tap Restore Purchases.

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. You can manage or cancel your subscription through your Apple ID/App Store account settings.

## Backups and Exports

Alined stores data locally on your device. If you want extra safety, use the built-in backup and export features before making major changes.

To create a full backup:

More → Backup & Restore → Export All

To restore from a saved backup:

More → Backup & Restore → Import Backup

Restoring a backup replaces the current local data in the app with the contents of the selected backup file.
